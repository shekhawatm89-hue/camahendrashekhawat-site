Refund policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy applies to the paid mentorship program for Class 11–12 Commerce, CA Foundation and CA Inter run by CA Mahendra Shekhawat ("we", "us").

7-day full refund

Join, go through your study audit and the first week of daily targets, and see the system for yourself. If you feel it is not right for you, ask for a refund within 7 days of the date you paid. We return the full fee. No questions, no deductions.

How to ask

  • Send a WhatsApp message to +91 94687 67477 with the student's name, course and the date of payment.
  • The request must be sent from the parent's or student's registered WhatsApp number.
  • The refund goes back to the same account or UPI ID the fee was paid from, within 7 working days of your request.

After 7 days

After the first 7 days, the fee is not refundable, because your seat (one of only 100) is reserved for you till your board exam or CA attempt and the mentoring work has started.

If we cannot continue

If we ever stop the program before your exam for a reason on our side, we refund the fee for the remaining months.

Questions

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 94687 67477.
