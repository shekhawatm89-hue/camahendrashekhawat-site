Refund policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy applies to the paid mentorship programs for Class 11–12 Commerce, CA Foundation and CA Inter run by CA Mahendra Shekhawat ("we", "us").

The rule

Full refund if requested within 7 days of payment. One-time fee, no partial refunds after that.

Join, go through your study audit and the first week of daily targets, and see the system for yourself. If it is not right for you, ask within 7 days of the date you paid and we return the full fee, with no deductions.

How to ask

  • Send a WhatsApp message to +91 94687 67477 with the student's name, course and the date of payment.
  • Send it from the parent's or student's registered WhatsApp number.
  • The refund goes back to the same account or UPI ID the fee was paid from, within 7 working days of your request.

Payment

After your enquiry we send a UPI / payment link on WhatsApp, and you get an invoice for the fee. The fee is paid once and covers the whole program.

Questions

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 94687 67477.
