Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what information we collect on camahendrashekhawat.com (the mentorship pages, the free study audit and the study app), why, and what you can ask us to do with it. Everything is run by CA Mahendra Shekhawat ("we", "us").

What we collect

  • "Join the mentorship" form: student name, class (on the Class 11–12 page) or course, WhatsApp number, and where you came from (the page, the video version, and the ad or campaign link if you used one).
  • Free study audit: name, age, school or institute (optional), city, course, attempt (for CA), target, plan after Class 12 (for Class 11–12), study hours on normal days and Sundays, how you study now, how far each chapter is done, your WhatsApp number, a parent's WhatsApp number (needed if you are under 18, with your parent's permission), your consent for WhatsApp messages, any feedback you send on the report, and the ad or campaign link you came from.
  • Study app (after joining): the joining form (student and parent WhatsApp numbers, school, marks in the last test, study hours, chapter progress), weekly plans, nightly check-ins (targets done, hours, a one-line note, and photos of your work if you choose to upload them), and anything you send through "Report a problem".
  • Login: we send a one-time code to your WhatsApp. If you use a PIN, it is stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form; we cannot see it.
  • On your device: the app and the audit keep your progress and a login token in your browser's storage so you don't have to retype. The pages also remember the campaign link you first came from for 30 days.
  • Visit statistics: on the public pages (mentorship pages, audit, policies) we use Meta Pixel and Google Analytics to count visits and clicks (for example WhatsApp, "Join", form sent, video played) and to measure our ads. These tools use cookies and see your browser and device details, not your answers. They are not used inside the study app.

Why we use it

  • To make your study audit report and your study plan.
  • So your mentor can follow your progress, check your tests, answer doubts and contact you.
  • To send WhatsApp messages: login codes, your report, reminders when a daily report is missed, and a weekly progress report to the parent. Stuck notes and photos are never sent to the parent.
  • To reply to your enquiry about joining.
  • To improve the program, using totals and averages without names.

We do not sell your information, and we never use your answers, marks or chapter details for advertising.

Students under 18

Many students are under 18. The joining form asks for a parent's WhatsApp number, and joining requires agreeing that we store the answers and send a weekly progress message to the parent. A parent can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete their child's information.

Where it is stored and who can see it

  • Your answers are stored in Google Sheets, and photos in Google Drive, in an account controlled by us. WhatsApp messages are sent through Meta's WhatsApp Business service.
  • Only CA Mahendra Shekhawat and mentors working with him can see individual student information.
  • We share information with others only if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you are in the program and for up to 12 months after your exam, so we can share results and plan next steps with you. Audit and enquiry details of students who do not join are kept for up to 12 months. After that we delete them, or keep only totals without names. You can ask us to delete your information earlier.

Your choices

  • Ask to see the information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct anything that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete your information and close your account. We will do this within 30 days.
  • Ask us to stop WhatsApp messages at any time.

Security

The site is served over a secure connection, PINs are stored hashed, and only our account can open the data sheets and photos. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't share your login code or PIN.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and tell current students on WhatsApp if the change matters to them.
