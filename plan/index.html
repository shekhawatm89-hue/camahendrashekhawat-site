      Day-by-day sheet

      Day
      • Can't do 12 hours?

        Keep the ratio 40 : 40 : 20. With 8 hours, that's about 3¼ hrs W1, 3¼ hrs W2 and 1½ hrs W3.

      • Test, then analyse

        After each test, spend at least 30 minutes on analysis: which topics you missed, and whether speed cost you marks.

      • Running behind?

        Don't skip the test. Take it on what you've finished, and add an extra hour from W3's time.

      • Phone in another room

        During the 5-hour blocks, keep it out of the room. Check it only in breaks.
